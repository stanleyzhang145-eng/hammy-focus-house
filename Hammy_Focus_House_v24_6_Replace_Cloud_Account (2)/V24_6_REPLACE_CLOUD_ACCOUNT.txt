HAMMY FOCUS HOUSE V24.6 — DELETE OLD ACCOUNT + FRESH CLOUD ACCOUNT

NEW DESTRUCTIVE ACCOUNT ACTION
Account now includes:
- Delete old account and create fresh account

WHAT IT DOES
1. Verifies the currently connected old cloud account.
2. Creates a completely fresh starter save.
3. Creates a new Player ID and recovery code.
4. Verifies that the new cloud account works.
5. Permanently deletes the old account.
6. Clears local old progress and Premium preview.
7. Switches the app to the new account.
8. Reloads and shows the new Player ID and recovery code.

FRESH ACCOUNT START
- White starter hamster
- 40 coins
- Starter fruit
- Starter clothing
- No purchased furniture
- No Premium entitlement
- No admin exclusives
- No old friends or public profile
- No old focus statistics

SAFETY
- Requires typing REPLACE and a second confirmation.
- The new account is verified before the old account is deleted.
- If old-account deletion fails, the unused new account is deleted as rollback.
- A pending replacement record protects against a browser interruption.
- If interruption happens after old deletion, the new account is recovered.
- If interruption happens before old deletion, the unused new account is removed.
- The old recovery backup is removed after successful permanent deletion.

IMPORTANT
This action cannot recover the old account after deletion.
