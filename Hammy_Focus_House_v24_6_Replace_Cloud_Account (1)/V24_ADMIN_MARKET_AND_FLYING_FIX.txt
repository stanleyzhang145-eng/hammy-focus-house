WHY THE HAMSTER WAS FLYING
v23 added this CSS rule:
#hamster { position: relative; }

The hamster already used absolute positioning inside the room. The new rule
overrode that and caused the hamster to move with the page layout. v24 removes
the incorrect rule and restores the floor position.
