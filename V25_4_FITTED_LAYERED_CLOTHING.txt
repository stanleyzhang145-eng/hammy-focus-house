HAMMY FOCUS HOUSE V25.4 — FITTED LAYERED CLOTHING

The flat rectangle clothing renderer was replaced with layered fitted garments.

- Shirts use curved torsos, collars, short sleeves, and a hem above the feet.
- Hoodies include a hood behind the head, strings, cuffs, pocket, and fitted sleeves.
- Pajamas include a fitted top, sleeves, cuffs, separate pants, and ankles above the feet.
- Jackets use open left and right panels, lapels, sleeves, and a visible belly gap.
- Capes attach behind the shoulders with a neck band and clasp.
- Hats follow the head curve and stay between the ears.
- Scarves, glasses, bows, crowns, backpacks, overalls, aprons, and Premium headbands use explicit layers.
- Face, cheeks, mouth, nose, eyes, and paws remain above clothing.
- Separate fit measurements support every breed.
- Cartoon-Cute is puffier; Realistic-Cute is slimmer.
- Clothing breathes and reacts to running and tickling.
- Home, clothing cards, and Premium rooms use the same renderer.
