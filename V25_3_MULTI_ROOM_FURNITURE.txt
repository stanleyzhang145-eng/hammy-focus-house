ROOM RULES
- Each room can hold six furniture pieces.
- Left and right arrow buttons appear at the top of the Home room.
- The room label shows the current room and furniture capacity.
- A placed furniture card shows which room contains it.
- Furniture can be moved to storage and placed again later.

PREMIUM
- When all generated rooms are full, placing another furniture piece
  automatically creates the next room.
- Premium can generate up to twelve rooms.
- The game switches directly to the newly generated room.

FREE
- Free players begin with Room 1.
- Room 2 unlocks after a 10-day consecutive focus streak.
- Before the streak is earned, a full room requires removing furniture.
- After Room 2 is full, free players must store furniture or unlock Premium.

COMPATIBILITY
- Old furniture saves migrate into rooms without deleting ownership.
- Existing furniture is distributed into available rooms.
- Extra furniture that cannot fit is moved safely into storage.
- Breed looks, cloud saving, admin gifts, Premium, clothing, and focus
  progress remain compatible.
